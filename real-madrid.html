History

Moments that built the club

  1. 1902
    Founding

    Madrid Football Club

    The club is founded on 6 March 1902 and plays in white from the start. That same year the team plays a national tournament in Madrid, and loses to Barcelona in the semi-final, the first meeting between the two clubs.

  2. 1920
    Royal title

    "Real" is added

    King Alfonso XIII grants the club the title Real, meaning royal. The crown has been part of the name ever since.

  3. 1947
    Stadium

    A new ground in Chamartín

    The stadium opens on 14 December 1947. It is renamed after president Santiago Bernabéu in 1955.

  4. 1953
    Signing

    Di Stéfano arrives

    Alfredo Di Stéfano joins from Argentina after a long dispute between Madrid and Barcelona over his rights. He becomes the centre of the greatest team of the decade.

  5. 1956–60
    Europe

    Five European Cups in a row

    Madrid wins the first five editions of the competition. The first final is a 4–3 win over Stade de Reims in Paris. The fifth is a 7–3 win over Eintracht Frankfurt at Hampden Park in 1960.

  6. 1966
    Europe

    A sixth, with a Spanish team

    A side built mostly from Spanish players beats Partizan Belgrade 2–1 in Brussels. The team becomes known as the Ye-yé generation.

  7. 1998
    Europe

    The seventh arrives after 32 years

    Predrag Mijatović scores the only goal against Juventus in Amsterdam.

  8. 2000, 2002
    Europe

    Two more in three years

    Madrid beat Valencia 3–0 in Paris in 2000. Two years later at Hampden Park, Zinedine Zidane volleys the winner in a 2–1 victory over Bayer Leverkusen.

  9. 2014
    Europe

    La Décima

    Sergio Ramos equalises in stoppage time of the final against Atlético Madrid in Lisbon. Madrid win 4–1 after extra time and finally reach ten European Cups.

  10. 2016–18
    Europe

    Three in a row

    Zinedine Zidane's team beats Atlético on penalties in 2016, Juventus 4–1 in Cardiff in 2017, and Liverpool 3–1 in Kyiv in 2018. No club had won three in a row since the competition became the Champions League in 1992.

  11. 2022
    Europe

    Fourteen, in Paris

    Vinícius Júnior scores the only goal against Liverpool at the Stade de France, after a season of late comebacks against Paris Saint-Germain, Chelsea and Manchester City.

  12. 2024
    Europe

    Fifteen, at Wembley

    Dani Carvajal and Vinícius Júnior score in a 2–0 win over Borussia Dortmund.

Home

Santiago Bernabéu

The stadium sits on the Paseo de la Castellana in the Chamartín district of Madrid. It has been rebuilt more than once, and the latest version has a roof that closes and a pitch that slides away.

Forward · 1953–1964

Led the team through all five of the first European Cups and set the standard for what a Madrid number 9 means.

Winger · 1953–1971

Rivals

Two fixtures that define a season

El Clásico

Real Madrid against Barcelona, the biggest fixture in Spanish football. The two clubs first met in 1902, and the rivalry drives the title race almost every season.

The Madrid derby

Real Madrid against Atlético, the neighbours across the city. Two Champions League finals have decided it: Lisbon in 2014 and Milan in 2016, both won by Real Madrid.
